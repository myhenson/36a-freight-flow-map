How to read this map

Planning context only. This concept is not a live traffic map or an official evacuation instruction. Follow local emergency-management guidance during an evacuation.

Highlighted highways use OpenStreetMap road geometry and route references in the CARTO vector basemap. Confirm official evacuation-route designations with TxDOT before publication. Traffic dots are a faster illustrative network animation—not observed traffic, evacuation direction, or a traffic count.

Colored moving indicators represent selected historical Gulf hurricane positions from NOAA/NHC HURDAT2. The looping display is for historical context, not a forecast, live storm, or evacuation alert. Distance rings are approximate and are not evacuation zones.

The 36A South study-area outline is approximate, traced from the published diagram; replace it with the project GIS boundary before publication.

Source diagram: Emergency Evacuation Routes Map · TxDOT project CSJ 0912-00-544, as cited in the diagram.

Historical storm tracks: NOAA/NHC HURDAT2 best-track archive. Selected storms: Rita (2005), Humberto (2007), Ike (2008), and Harvey (2017).
